What happens when you ask

The model never touches your file. It is handed a copy of what is relevant and returns text into a panel; the only thing that writes to disk is you, accepting something. Step through any of the three paths below.

    Why the model cannot write

    There is no path from Claude to your file. Output becomes a row in invocations and a card in the panel; applying it takes a diff you confirmed. The cost of a bad suggestion is a glance, not a revert.

    Why canon goes in whole

    Retrieval is how you search a corpus that grows. But the documents that define the work are few and load-bearing, and a search that misses one is a model contradicting your own rules. Those go in entire, every call, on purpose.
